Pain points

Messaging feels similar to generic research repositories.

ROI is hard to judge before seeing real examples from a similar company.

Connection requirements create setup anxiety and delay the aha moment.

Teams worry about whether generated insights are traceable to evidence.

Ownership model is unclear once multiple teams edit the map.

Opportunities

Design decisions

  • Horizontal stage layout matches how teams workshop journey maps in a room.
  • Sticky row labels keep context while scanning a large evidence matrix.
  • Emotion meters make weak moments visible without overloading the board.
  • Priority insight panel gives executives a summary before they inspect details.
